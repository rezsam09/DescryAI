Descry AI

An 8-week remote AI safety research program for talented high school students in the Global South.

See the program

What we do

We pair AI safety researchers with motivated students who would otherwise have little access to research. Students carry out real projects and work toward submitting them to workshops at top AI conferences.

Mission

To expand AI safety research capacity in the Global South by pairing AI safety researchers in the field with talented and motivated mentees who go on to lead the next generation of alignment work.

Vision

A global AI safety ecosystem where talent in the Global South has access to meaningful high-impact research to spot emerging risks early and build the global mobility and influence needed to lead critical safety work unhindered by geographic barriers.
