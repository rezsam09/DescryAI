Program overview

An 8-week AI safety research program for talented high schoolers in the Global South with the opportunity to collaborate with peers and learn from mentors.

Collaborate with peers and mentors

Work alongside experienced AI safety researchers who provide feedback, technical support, and paper reviews. Brainstorm, solve problems, and co-author projects with a talented cohort of students from the Global South.

Execute your AI safety research

We provide the compute resources and organizational backing necessary to bring your AI safety ideas to fruition. Beyond project execution, our program offers tailored publication assistance to help you make an impact in the field.

Make an impact

We empower talented students across the Global South to lead the next generation of AI safety research and to join a growing ecosystem of researchers dedicated to building resilience against AI risks.

Program timeline

  1. Week 0Prerequisites and project overview, led by your mentor
  2. Weeks 1-2Literature review and project plan outline
  3. Weeks 3-4Environment setup and experiments
  4. Weeks 5-6Experimentation and initial results
  5. Weeks 7-8Scaling and write-up
