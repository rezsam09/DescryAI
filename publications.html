Publications

Descry AI aims to bring local AI safety work from the Global South to the attention of the broader research community by publishing in targeted workshops at top AI conferences such as NeurIPS, ACL, and CVPR.

These workshops are primarily organized by PhD students and professional researchers interested in the specific subfield, which provides networking opportunities for student and early-career researchers seeking feedback and collaborators in the field. Our goal is to connect promising talent into these circles early on to maximize their future impact and ensure Global South perspectives play a meaningful role in shaping AI safety research.
